# Child's Path — official source review

**Reviewed:** 21 September 2026  
**Scope:** Claims in `packages/shared/pathways-catalogue.ts` plus the board
ladders used on Child's Path. Not a legal opinion. Not college-by-college
admission advice.

**Verdict:** The **options** (what can come next on each board) are real.
The **exam names and Class 12-equivalent gates** match 2026 official
bulletins. A few **URLs were stale**. Dates, domicile, and “this child can
apply” stay **Needs verification** at counselling time.

## Still not “validated as Eligible”

Keep these as orientation, never as a green Eligible badge:

1. **This child’s school offers** DP / A Level / CBSE 11–12. Switch cards
   are possible routes, not school inventory.
2. **Domicile / local vs non-local** for TG EAPCET or POLYCET.
3. **Subject level:** NTA lists IB Diploma and A Level as *qualifying
   exams*. It does **not** say “any three HL subjects.” Engineering still
   needs Physics and Maths plus an allowed science/vocational subject in
   the bulletin. NEET still needs PCB + English. Confirm on that year’s
   form.
4. **Calendar collision** (May DP vs NEET/JEE/EAPCET) is a planning
   warning. Exact dates move every year.
5. **IBCP** vs Indian UG: still case-by-case. Do not treat as DP.
6. **IOQM “middle school onward”** is slightly loose: 2026 rules are
   Class 8–12 with a birth-date window.
7. **JEE “PCM”** is the parent intent label. Bulletin Paper 1 allows
   Physics + Maths + Chemistry *or* Biotech/Biology/Technical Vocational.

## Board options (ladders) — not assumptions

These are how the boards are organised. Official sites describe the
programmes; schools choose what they offer.

| Family | Ladder we show | Official basis |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| CBSE | 10 → 11–12 subject combinations → 12 | CBSE secondary / senior secondary |
| Cambridge | IGCSE (Class 10-stage) → A Level (Class 12-stage in NTA/AIU lists) | Cambridge International; NTA lists **A Level**, not IGCSE, as +2 |
| IB | PYP → MYP (Class 10-stage) → DP (Class 12-stage) | IBO programmes; AIU as above |
| SSC TG/AP | 10 → Intermediate or POLYCET | BSE Telangana / BIE + SBTET |
| ICSE | 10 → ISC 11–12 | CISCE. **Not** a seeded child curriculum in our DB yet |
