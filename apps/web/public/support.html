Help & Support

This is the support page for the Vaara Parents mobile app. Email us for account help, privacy questions, or community safety concerns.

Email support

support@vaara.ai

Include your registered email, device type (iPhone or Android), and a short description of the issue. Do not send child names, photos, chat contents, or passwords.

Email support@vaara.ai

What we can help with

  • Sign-in, account access, and profile updates
  • Privacy questions and data-deletion requests
  • Reports about posts, messages, or other parents
  • Bugs, crashes, and other technical issues

In the app

  • Open More → Help & Support for FAQs and the same email action.
  • Report a post, chat, or parent from the ⋯ menu, then choose a reason.
  • Delete your account from Settings & Privacy → Delete account, or use our account deletion page.

Not an emergency service

Vaara is not an emergency service. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
