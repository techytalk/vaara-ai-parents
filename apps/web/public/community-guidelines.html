Community Guidelines

Effective date: September 10, 2026 · App: Vaara Parents

Vaara Parents is a private community for adult parents and caregivers to share school, class, and neighborhood information. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, messages, marketplace listings, and any other content you share.

We filter posts that violate these rules, and we review reports. You can report content or a parent, and you can block someone to stop future contact. For safety questions, email support@vaara.ai.

Who may use Vaara

  • The app is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13.
  • Use your own account. Do not impersonate another parent, school, or tutor.
  • Keep child identities private. Use nicknames, not full names of children, in public circles.

What is not allowed

  • Sexual, pornographic, or sexually explicit content
  • Hate speech, slurs, bullying, or harassment
  • Threats, violent content, or encouragement of self-harm
  • Spam, scams, or misleading commercial posts
  • Content that exploits or endangers children
  • Dosing or medical advice between parents (ask a qualified professional)

How to report and block

  • Open the ⋯ menu on a post, conversation, or connection request and choose Report.
  • Choose a reason so our team can review it. We aim to act on safety reports promptly.
  • You can Block a parent from a chat or connection request to stop future contact.
  • Vaara is not an emergency service. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

How we enforce these rules

We may remove content, restrict features, or delete accounts that violate these guidelines, our privacy policy, or the law. Automated filters may block some posts before they are published. Human review follows reports.

Contact

Community safety and guideline questions: support@vaara.ai
